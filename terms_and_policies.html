Refund & Cancellation Policy

12-hour eligibility window, deduction formulas & gateway terms

12-Hr Window

🕒 12-Hour Cancellation Eligibility Window

Standard (Normal) Application cancellation is eligible "before 12 hours from purchase OR before our agent's 1st call attempt, whichever is earlier."

📊 Pre-Filing / 1st Agent Call Attempt Deduction

Formula: 25% of application fee OR ₹30, whichever is HIGHER.

• Example 1 (₹100 Application Fee): 25% of ₹100 is ₹25, but ₹30 is higher, so ₹30 is deducted and ₹70 is refunded. • Example 2 (₹200 Application Fee): 25% of ₹200 is ₹50 (which is higher than ₹30), so ₹50 is deducted and ₹150 is refunded.

📞 2nd Agent Call Attempt Deduction

Formula: 50% of application fee OR ₹40, whichever is HIGHER.

• Example (₹100 Application Fee): 50% of ₹100 is ₹50 (higher than ₹40), so ₹50 is deducted and ₹50 is refunded.

🚫 Post-Filing Policy (After Application Uploaded to Portal)

Once an application has been processed and submitted to official destination, entrance exam, or university servers, official organization fees become non-refundable (100% deduction - 0% refund) as official servers do not issue reversals.

💳 Payment Gateway Processing Fee (Non-Refundable)

An additional payment processing charge starting at 2.5% (or higher depending on payment gateway/method) is added to each transaction. This fee is strictly non-refundable under any circumstances, even if an application is cancelled or refunded.

⚡ Instant Applications (Non-Refundable)

Instant applications align dedicated processing agents immediately on an urgent basis. This incurs higher operational cost and leads to 100% NO REFUND once purchase is completed.

🔄 Application Re-filing Policy

If an application needs re-filing, a new application form must be submitted. Users can request a fee waiver or discount by emailing support, subject to operational review.

💳 Refund Timelines & Processing

We initiate refunds within 12 to 24 working hours after applying cancellation fee deductions. Final credit reflection in your account may take longer due to bank servers or payment gateway processing.
